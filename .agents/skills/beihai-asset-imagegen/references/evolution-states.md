# Evolution States

Each spirit has 11 image states. Lv.1 is the egg period and is represented by four internal states. Lv.2 is the first fully hatched young spirit.

| State | XP range | Display meaning | Art requirement |
|---|---:|---|---|
| egg-1 | 0-24 | Complete egg | Distinct egg with motif, no cracks |
| egg-2 | 25-49 | Slightly cracked egg | Same egg with small cracks and inner glow |
| egg-3 | 50-74 | Partial reveal | Same egg with a small eye, ear, horn, leaf, tail, or light peeking out |
| egg-4 | 75-99 | Head peeking out | Same egg with the spirit's head or clear key feature emerging |
| lv2 | 100-249 | Fully hatched young spirit | Original small rounded companion, previous Lv.1 concept |
| lv3 | 250-449 | Brighter young spirit | Same spirit with richer color and a small light or charm |
| lv4 | 450-699 | Virtue-marked spirit | Adds virtue emblem, magic pattern, or symbolic ornament |
| lv5 | 700-999 | Refined spirit | Body details become more elegant and polished |
| lv6 | 1000-1399 | Equipped spirit | Adds non-weapon companion equipment such as bell, book, leaf cape, crystal, ribbon, or badge |
| lv7 | 1400-1899 | Aura spirit | Adds orbiting aura, star ring, leaf trail, glow ribbon, or elemental halo |
| lv8 | 1900+ | Guardian spirit | Strongest first-version form, still cute, gentle, and child-safe |

## Continuity Rules

- The same spirit must retain its core silhouette, eyes, motif, and palette family across all 11 states.
- Evolution should add refinement, not replace the character with an unrelated design.
- Higher levels may be more elaborate, but must not become aggressive, adult, armored, or weaponized.
- Lv.8 may look guardian-like, but should still feel like a trusted companion.
